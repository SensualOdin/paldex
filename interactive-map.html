Features other maps charge for

Unlimited found-tracking — check off chests, effigies and eggs with per-type progress, no account and no marker cap. Custom pins with notes (double-click anywhere). Export / import your progress as a file.

Pal spawn layers — overlay up to three color-coded habitat heat maps at once with a day/night toggle, or click any empty spot to see exactly which Pals spawn there, straight from the game's spawn-distribution table.

Shareable location links — every marker popup gives a link (like #map=main/337/360) that opens the map at that exact spot; popups show Alpha Pal mini-stats and the nearest fast travel statue with distance.

Alpha Pal popups deep-link to their Paldex pages; resource nodes link to the item database so you can see what each material crafts into.
